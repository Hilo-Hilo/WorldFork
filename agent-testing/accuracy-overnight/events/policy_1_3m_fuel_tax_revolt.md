# Benchmark Event policy_1_3m_fuel_tax_revolt

    Category: policy/regulatory backlash

    Horizon: 1-3 months

    Anonymization rule: this dossier intentionally removes real names, organizations, locations, dates that uniquely identify the original event, and direct outcome facts. Source and outcome material is kept only in `sources/policy_1_3m_fuel_tax_revolt.json`.

    ## Start-State Scenario

    A government frames a fuel-tax increase as climate policy, but commuters and rural residents organize visible protest around cost-of-living pressure.

    ## Situation at T0

    The movement is decentralized, disruptive, and hard to satisfy with a narrow tax adjustment because broader inequality grievances surface.

    ## Stakeholders

    - national government
- rural commuters
- urban protesters
- police
- small businesses

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - fuel prices
- elite legitimacy
- road blockades
- broad grievance expansion

    ## Constraints

    - the tax can be suspended quickly
- movement leaders are diffuse
- concessions may invite further demands

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether tax suspension ends protests
- whether violence polarizes opinion
- whether broader concessions follow

    Simulate plausible social, institutional, market, and legitimacy dynamics from this start state. Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing a single endpoint early.
