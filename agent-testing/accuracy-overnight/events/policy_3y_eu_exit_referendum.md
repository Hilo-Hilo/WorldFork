# Benchmark Event policy_3y_eu_exit_referendum

    Category: policy/regulatory backlash

    Horizon: 3+ years

    Anonymization rule: this dossier intentionally removes real names, organizations, locations, dates that uniquely identify the original event, and direct outcome facts. Source and outcome material is kept only in `sources/policy_3y_eu_exit_referendum.json`.

    ## Start-State Scenario

    A country votes narrowly to leave a supranational union, triggering party leadership changes, negotiation deadlines, and deep public division.

    ## Situation at T0

    The process requires withdrawal legislation, treaty negotiation, parliamentary votes, and choices about economic alignment.

    ## Stakeholders

    - national government
- parliament
- supranational negotiators
- businesses
- voters

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - referendum mandate
- party splits
- trade friction
- identity polarization

    ## Constraints

    - the vote creates political pressure but not an instant exit
- negotiations expose tradeoffs
- deadlines can be extended

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether exit is reversed
- whether a negotiated deal passes
- whether no-deal rupture occurs

    ## Simulation Instruction

    Simulate plausible social, institutional, market, and legitimacy dynamics from this start state. Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing a single endpoint early.
