# Benchmark Event campus_3y_movement_against_harassment

    Category: campus/civil society

    Horizon: 3+ years

    Anonymization rule: this dossier intentionally removes real names, organizations, locations, dates that uniquely identify the original event, and direct outcome facts. Source and outcome material is kept only in `sources/campus_3y_movement_against_harassment.json`.

    ## Start-State Scenario

    Survivors and journalists expose sexual harassment and assault allegations against powerful figures, creating a viral civil-society accountability wave.

    ## Situation at T0

    Institutions must decide whether to investigate, remove leaders, revise policies, or wait for attention to fade.

    ## Stakeholders

    - survivors
- journalists
- employers
- courts
- advocacy groups

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - networked testimony
- workplace liability
- reputational contagion
- backlash over due process

    ## Constraints

    - legal standards vary
- public accusations can move faster than courts
- policy change is uneven across sectors

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether disclosures sustain momentum
- whether backlash narrows reforms
- whether formal law and workplace practice shift

    ## Simulation Instruction

    Simulate plausible social, institutional, market, and legitimacy dynamics from this start state. Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing a single endpoint early. Do not assume the real-world case or import named facts from outside this dossier. When branches are warranted, branch on materially different endpoint mechanisms, not only on tone or messaging.
