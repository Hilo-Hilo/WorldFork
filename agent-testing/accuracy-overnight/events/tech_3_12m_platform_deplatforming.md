    The app loses mainstream distribution and hosting at once, while supporters seek alternative infrastructure and critics demand stronger moderation.

- political users

- alternative hosting scramble

    ## Constraints

- migration requires technical rebuild
- public attention is intense but may decay

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

- whether app stores re-admit the service
- whether users return after downtime
