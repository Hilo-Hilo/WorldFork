    ## Start-State Scenario

    A large online community platform announces a sudden paid access regime for a developer interface used by third-party clients and volunteer moderation tools.

    ## Situation at T0

    Volunteer moderators coordinate a short service blackout while platform executives signal that monetization and control of external clients are non-negotiable.

    ## Stakeholders

    - platform executives
- volunteer moderators
- third-party developers
- heavy users
- advertisers

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - loss of moderator trust
- developer exit threats
- advertiser sensitivity
- platform dependence by communities

    ## Constraints

    - the platform owns the infrastructure
- moderators have symbolic leverage but limited formal authority
- users have switching costs

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether the blackout lasts beyond the first week
- whether administrators replace moderators
- whether accessibility or moderation exceptions are granted
