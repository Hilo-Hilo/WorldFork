# Benchmark Event finance_3_12m_algorithmic_stablecoin_collapse

    Category: finance/market confidence

    Horizon: 3-12 months

    Anonymization rule: this dossier intentionally removes real names, organizations, locations, dates that uniquely identify the original event, and direct outcome facts. Source and outcome material is kept only in `sources/finance_3_12m_algorithmic_stablecoin_collapse.json`.

    ## Start-State Scenario

    An algorithmic stablecoin ecosystem promises dollar stability through a linked volatile token and very high yields.

    ## Situation at T0

    A de-peg triggers reflexive redemptions, collapsing confidence in the mechanism and spreading losses across crypto markets.

    ## Stakeholders

    - stablecoin issuer
- token holders
- exchanges
- lending platforms
- regulators

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - reflexive death spiral
- yield dependence
- liquidity exit
- regulatory scrutiny

    ## Constraints

    - the peg depends on confidence
- reserves are limited relative to panic
- contagion can hit exposed funds and lenders

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether the peg is restored
- whether ecosystem token collapses
- whether enforcement follows
