    Donors, faculty, students, trustees, media, and politicians contest whether the president can remain legitimate.

- governing board
- donors
- faculty
- political critics

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - campus speech crisis
- donor leverage
- plagiarism allegations
- board confidence

    ## Constraints

    - the board can initially defend leadership
- media cycles can compound issues
- resignation may not resolve underlying campus conflict

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether board support holds
- whether new allegations accumulate
- whether resignation becomes the least costly endpoint
