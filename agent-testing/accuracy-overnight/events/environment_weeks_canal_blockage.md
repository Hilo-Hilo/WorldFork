    ## Start-State Scenario

    A giant container ship becomes lodged sideways in a narrow global trade chokepoint, halting traffic in both directions.

    ## Situation at T0

    Salvage crews race tides, dredging capacity, tug coordination, and mounting shipping delays while global media watches minute by minute.

    ## Stakeholders

    - canal authority
- ship operator
- salvage teams
- shipping firms
- insurers

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - trade bottleneck
- technical salvage uncertainty
- insurance claims
- media spectacle

    ## Constraints

    - the obstruction is physical not political
- traffic queues grow daily
- refloating depends on tides and equipment

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether refloating takes days or weeks
- whether cargo must be unloaded
- whether legal detention follows
