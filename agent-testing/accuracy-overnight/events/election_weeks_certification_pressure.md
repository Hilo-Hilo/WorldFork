# Benchmark Event election_weeks_certification_pressure

    Category: elections/legitimacy

    Horizon: weeks

    Anonymization rule: this dossier intentionally removes real names, organizations, locations, dates that uniquely identify the original event, and direct outcome facts. Source and outcome material is kept only in `sources/election_weeks_certification_pressure.json`.

    ## Start-State Scenario

    An incumbent loses a national election and allies pressure courts, local officials, and legislative actors to reject certified results.

    ## Situation at T0

    The losing side claims fraud without enough evidence, while decentralized certification rules and courts create many veto points against reversal.

    ## Stakeholders

    - incumbent coalition
- winning coalition
- courts
- state election officials
- legislators

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - elite pressure
- misinformation
- formal certification deadlines
- institutional independence

    ## Constraints

    - state results are certified separately
- courts require evidence
- the final transfer has a constitutional date

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether courts intervene
- whether state officials defect
- whether violence changes certification

    ## Simulation Instruction

    Simulate plausible social, institutional, market, and legitimacy dynamics from this start state. Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing a single endpoint early.
