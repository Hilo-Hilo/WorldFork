    ## Start-State Scenario

    A welfare agency automates debt notices using income averaging, producing large numbers of disputed debts against benefit recipients.

    ## Situation at T0

    Legal aid groups, affected citizens, journalists, and courts pressure the government to admit the method is unlawful.

    ## Stakeholders

    - welfare agency
- ministers
- benefit recipients
- courts
- public auditors

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - budget-recovery incentives
- citizen hardship
- legal uncertainty
- administrative denial

    ## Constraints

    - many debts are already collected
- the agency controls records
- courts can force settlement
- political accountability may lag

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether debts are paused
- whether compensation is paid
- whether a public inquiry follows
