    Civil society groups challenge the system as opaque, discriminatory, and incompatible with rights protections.

- court

- poverty stigma
- fraud-control pressure

    ## Constraints

    - the system depends on legal authority
- affected citizens lack individual transparency
- courts can halt deployment

- whether the system is paused or banned
