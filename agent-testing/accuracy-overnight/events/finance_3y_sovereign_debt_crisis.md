# Benchmark Event finance_3y_sovereign_debt_crisis

    Category: finance/market confidence

    Horizon: 3+ years

    Anonymization rule: this dossier intentionally removes real names, organizations, locations, dates that uniquely identify the original event, and direct outcome facts. Source and outcome material is kept only in `sources/finance_3y_sovereign_debt_crisis.json`.

    ## Start-State Scenario

    A euro-area member state faces unsustainable debt, austerity demands, bank stress, public protests, and recurring bailout negotiations.

    ## Situation at T0

    Domestic democratic mandates clash with creditor conditions and fears that exit from a currency union would create wider contagion.

    ## Stakeholders

    - national government
- euro-area creditors
- IMF
- banks
- voters

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - austerity fatigue
- bank run risk
- currency-union contagion
- referendum mandate

    ## Constraints

    - creditors can withhold funding
- exit is technically and politically risky
- bailouts can extend crisis without resolving all debt burdens

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether the country exits the currency union
- whether bailout terms are accepted
- whether debt relief follows

    ## Simulation Instruction

    Simulate plausible social, institutional, market, and legitimacy dynamics from this start state. Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing a single endpoint early. Do not assume the real-world case or import named facts from outside this dossier. When branches are warranted, branch on materially different endpoint mechanisms, not only on tone or messaging.
