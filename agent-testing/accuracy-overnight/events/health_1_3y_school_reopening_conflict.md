    ## Start-State Scenario

    A respiratory pandemic forces school closures, remote learning, mitigation debates, and conflict over when and how to reopen.

    ## Situation at T0

    Public health risks, learning loss, parent labor constraints, teacher safety, and local politics pull decisions in different directions.

    ## Stakeholders

    - school districts
- teachers unions
- parents
- public health agencies
- students

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - learning loss
- infection risk
- mask fatigue
- local political polarization

    ## Constraints

    - authority is decentralized
- variants change risk perception
- vaccines reduce but do not eliminate conflict

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether closures persist
- whether mitigations stabilize reopening
- whether politics overrides health guidance
