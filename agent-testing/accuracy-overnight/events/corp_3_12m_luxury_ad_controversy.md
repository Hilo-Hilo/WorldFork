    ## Start-State Scenario

    A luxury brand releases campaign imagery that critics interpret as sexualizing children and associating products with disturbing legal documents.

    ## Situation at T0

    The brand initially blames production partners but public outrage targets creative governance and corporate responsibility.

    ## Stakeholders

    - luxury brand
- creative agencies
- parents
- fashion customers
- celebrity ambassadors

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - moral shock
- visual evidence
- apology credibility
- lawsuit optics

    ## Constraints

    - campaigns can be pulled quickly
- luxury demand may recover if controversy fades
- blame shifting can worsen trust

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether the brand accepts responsibility
- whether lawsuits continue
- whether ambassadors distance themselves
