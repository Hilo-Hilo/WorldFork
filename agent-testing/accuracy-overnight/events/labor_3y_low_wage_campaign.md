    ## Start-State Scenario

    Fast-food and low-wage workers launch a campaign around a simple wage demand that initially seems far above prevailing minimums.

    ## Situation at T0

    The movement relies on strikes, moral framing, city and state policy wins, and long-term normalization rather than one federal bargaining table.

    ## Stakeholders

    - low-wage workers
- labor organizers
- city councils
- state governments
- national parties

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - simple wage frame
- local policy venues
- employer lobbying
- long-run public opinion shift

    ## Constraints

    - federal law is hard to change
- cities and states can move first
- inflation changes the perceived ambition of the target

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether the slogan diffuses
- whether local wins aggregate
- whether a federal floor changes
