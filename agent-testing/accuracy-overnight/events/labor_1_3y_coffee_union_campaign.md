# Benchmark Event labor_1_3y_coffee_union_campaign

    Category: labor/social movements

    Horizon: 1-3 years

    Anonymization rule: this dossier intentionally removes real names, organizations, locations, dates that uniquely identify the original event, and direct outcome facts. Source and outcome material is kept only in `sources/labor_1_3y_coffee_union_campaign.json`.

    ## Start-State Scenario

    Workers at a flagship retail chain begin store-by-store union elections, creating a symbolic challenge to a company known for progressive branding.

    ## Situation at T0

    The campaign spreads quickly but bargaining is slow, legal disputes multiply, and the company can contest each store separately.

    ## Stakeholders

    - store workers
- national union
- corporate leadership
- labor board
- customers

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - brand contradiction
- store-level fragmentation
- legal unfair-labor-practice claims
- first-contract difficulty

    ## Constraints

    - each workplace votes separately
- the company controls scheduling and messaging
- public support does not automatically create contracts

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether union wins convert into contracts
- whether labor board remedies matter
- whether company chooses a national path
