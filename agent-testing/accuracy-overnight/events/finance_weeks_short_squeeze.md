# Benchmark Event finance_weeks_short_squeeze

    Category: finance/market confidence

    Horizon: weeks

    Anonymization rule: this dossier intentionally removes real names, organizations, locations, dates that uniquely identify the original event, and direct outcome facts. Source and outcome material is kept only in `sources/finance_weeks_short_squeeze.json`.

    ## Start-State Scenario

    Retail traders coordinate around a heavily shorted stock, producing extreme price volatility and pressure on brokers and clearing systems.

    ## Situation at T0

    Broker restrictions, social-media coordination, hedge-fund losses, and regulatory scrutiny interact over a very compressed time frame.

    ## Stakeholders

    - retail traders
- short sellers
- brokerage platforms
- clearinghouse
- regulators

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - short interest
- viral coordination
- collateral requirements
- market fairness narratives

    ## Constraints

    - brokers must meet clearing deposits
- retail attention can reverse quickly
- hearings can follow without changing the immediate price path

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether restrictions trigger distrust
- whether price holds
- whether systemic market failure occurs

    ## Simulation Instruction

    Simulate plausible social, institutional, market, and legitimacy dynamics from this start state. Preserve uncertainty rather than forcing a single endpoint early. Do not assume the real-world case or import named facts from outside this dossier. When branches are warranted, branch on materially different endpoint mechanisms, not only on tone or messaging.
