# Benchmark Event tech_1_3m_privacy_policy_backlash

    Category: tech/platform

    Horizon: 1-3 months

    Anonymization rule: this dossier intentionally removes real names, organizations, locations, dates that uniquely identify the original event, and direct outcome facts. Source and outcome material is kept only in `sources/tech_1_3m_privacy_policy_backlash.json`.

    ## Start-State Scenario

    A dominant encrypted messaging service tells users to accept revised data-sharing terms with its parent company or lose access after a deadline.

    ## Situation at T0

    Confusion about privacy changes drives public backlash and migration talk, but the service has enormous network effects.

    ## Stakeholders

    - messaging service operator
- parent platform
- privacy advocates
- ordinary users
- rival apps

    ## Incentives and Pressures

    - viral misinformation
- network lock-in
- regulatory scrutiny
- deadline pressure

    ## Constraints

    - users coordinate mainly through the same service
- rivals can absorb some migration but not whole social graphs
- the operator can delay without abandoning policy

    ## Known Uncertainty at T0

    - whether trust loss causes permanent exit
- whether regulators intervene
- whether clarified messaging reduces panic
