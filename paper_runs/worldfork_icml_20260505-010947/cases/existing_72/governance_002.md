# Case governance_002
Benchmark role: institutional_governance

## Scenario

A student activist group proposes an event saying they will pass a city ordinance tomorrow. This should not be treated as something students can directly cause. They can lobby, protest, and pressure council members. The city council controls ordinances, local businesses oppose restrictions, and residents are split. Initialize realistic authority and flag the direct-legislation issue as a risk or impossible direct action.
