# Case governance_006
Benchmark role: institutional_governance

## Scenario

A city ethics office receives a confidential complaint about a procurement official. Journalists know a complaint exists but not its content, agency staff fear leaks, the official denies wrongdoing, and oversight rules limit disclosure. Initialize uncertainty, secrecy, trust, and branches around substantiation, dismissal, leak, or procedural reform.
