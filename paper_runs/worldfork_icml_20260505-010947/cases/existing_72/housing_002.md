# Case housing_002
Benchmark role: housing_zoning

## Scenario

A city emergency-housing program leases a hotel for families displaced by fires. Nearby residents complain about safety, service providers cite urgent need, hotel workers worry about job changes, and courts may review zoning objections. Model authority, dependency, stigma, and endpoints around continued lease, relocation, injunction, or service expansion.
