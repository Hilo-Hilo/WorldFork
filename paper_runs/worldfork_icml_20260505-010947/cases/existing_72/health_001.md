# Case health_001
Benchmark role: public_health_resource

## Scenario

A fictional county health office announces a cluster of stomach illness cases possibly linked to a public pool, but lab confirmation is pending. Parents share rumors, pool staff fear reputational harm, doctors want clear guidance, and county officials must avoid overclaiming. Initialize uncertainty, trust edges, fear/confusion observations, and branches around confirmation, false alarm, or delayed correction.

- institutional_trust
