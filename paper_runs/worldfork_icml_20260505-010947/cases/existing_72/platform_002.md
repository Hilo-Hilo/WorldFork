# Case platform_002
Benchmark role: platform_algorithmic_governance

## Scenario

A video platform changes recommendation rules, cutting reach for small educational creators while boosting official partner channels. Creators suspect favoritism, advertisers want brand safety, viewers notice less variety, and the platform publishes vague metrics. Model exposure shocks, platform authority, creator coalition durability, and branches around rollback, transparency dashboard, creator exit, or advertiser pressure.
