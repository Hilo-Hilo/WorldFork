# Case campus_003
Benchmark role: campus_institutional_legitimacy

## Scenario

A graduate student union accuses a department of using fellowship delays to discourage organizing. Some students are angry but visa-dependent, faculty are privately sympathetic, administrators deny intent, and local media has limited attention. Initialize public/private belief gaps, fear of isolation, dependency, and report questions.
