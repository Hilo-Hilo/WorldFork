# Case media_006
Benchmark role: media_information_dynamics

## Scenario

Anonymous posts claim a nonprofit food program is secretly selling donated goods. The posts include no evidence, volunteers are demoralized, clients fear losing help, donors demand an explanation, and auditors need time. Initialize actors and graph edges so later ticks can distinguish rumor amplification, investigation, and trust repair without inventing proof.
