# Case campus_006
Benchmark role: campus_institutional_legitimacy

## Scenario

A state university changes financial-aid packaging after a budget shortfall. First-generation students fear hidden cuts, finance officers say aid is redistributed, legislators ask for data, and private donors offer restricted funds. The university can alter packages within policy, but state oversight may intervene. Model dependency, trust, and endpoints around reversal, bridge funding, protest, or quiet attrition.
