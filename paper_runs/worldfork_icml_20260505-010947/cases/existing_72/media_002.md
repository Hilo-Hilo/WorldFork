# Case media_002
Benchmark role: media_information_dynamics

## Scenario

An edited video appears to show a transit officer insulting riders, but full footage is unavailable. Rider groups, transit police, local influencers, and investigative reporters respond. The agency can release body-camera footage after privacy review, influencers can amplify but not verify facts, and rumors may persist after correction. Seed evidence uncertainty and correction mechanisms.

## Expected Focus
- misinformation
- network_exposure
- authority
