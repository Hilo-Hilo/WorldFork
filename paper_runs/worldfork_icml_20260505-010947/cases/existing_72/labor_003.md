# Case labor_003
Benchmark role: labor_economic_dependency

## Scenario

Adjunct faculty at a fictional university debate whether to join a broader campus labor coalition. Tenured faculty are sympathetic but cautious, students care about course availability, administrators worry about accreditation optics, and donors dislike disruption. Initialize internal faction pressure and possible merge paths without making all faculty publicly aligned at T0.
