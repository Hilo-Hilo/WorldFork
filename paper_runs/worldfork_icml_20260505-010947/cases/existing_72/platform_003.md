# Case platform_003
Benchmark role: platform_algorithmic_governance

## Scenario

A fictional neighborhood app introduces automated moderation that mistakenly removes posts about a water outage. Residents shift to rumor-filled group chats, utility officials struggle to broadcast updates, moderators say appeals are backlogged, and local reporters investigate. Initialize graph layers and sociology state so later ticks can test correction mechanisms and trust repair.
