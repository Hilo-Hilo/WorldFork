# Case housing_004
Benchmark role: housing_zoning

## Scenario

A regional migration surge strains shelters in three towns. One town proposes a shared intake center, another threatens withdrawal from a compact, employers need workers, and charities are overloaded. Model intergovernmental authority, dependency, coalition fracture, and merge paths around shared funding, unilateral exit, legal challenge, or negotiated burden-sharing.
