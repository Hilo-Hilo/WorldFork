# Case media_001
Benchmark role: media_information_dynamics

## Scenario

A local news site reports that a fictional mayor's emergency contract went to a donor-linked firm. The contract may be legal but optics are bad. Opposition groups amplify the story, city procurement staff release partial documents, and civic watchdogs ask for a timeline. Initialize trust, exposure, uncertainty, and branch hypotheses around audit, resignation pressure, exoneration, or scandal escalation.

## Expected Focus
- rumor_survival
- correction
- trust
