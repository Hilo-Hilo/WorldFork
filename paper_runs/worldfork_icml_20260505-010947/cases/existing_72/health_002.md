# Case health_002
Benchmark role: public_health_resource

## Scenario

A regional drought authority proposes tiered water rationing. Farmers, suburban homeowners, industrial users, urban tenants, and environmental advocates all claim fairness. The authority can set rationing rules, courts can review later, and black-market water hauling rumors emerge. Model resource dependency, compliance pressure, public silence, and endpoints around strict rationing, exemptions, enforcement backlash, or negotiated conservation.
