# Case media_004
Benchmark role: media_information_dynamics

## Scenario

A regional newspaper paywalls election coverage two weeks before a local referendum. Civic groups accuse it of reducing access, the publisher cites financial survival, campaigns adapt messaging, and social media snippets distort articles. Model exposure edges, information asymmetry, and endpoints around paywall rollback, public backlash, subscription defense, or misinformation spread.
