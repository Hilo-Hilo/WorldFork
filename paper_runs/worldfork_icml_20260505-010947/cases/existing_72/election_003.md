# Case election_003
Benchmark role: election_legitimacy

## Scenario

After a fictional mayoral debate, a candidate's ambiguous answer about emergency shelters goes viral. Advocacy groups demand clarification, campaign staff issue partial corrections, reporters fact-check the quote, and undecided voters are fatigued. Initialize exposure, trust, campaign incentives, and branch hypotheses around clarification, backlash, distraction, or issue reframing.
