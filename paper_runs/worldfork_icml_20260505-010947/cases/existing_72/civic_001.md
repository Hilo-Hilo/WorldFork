# Case civic_001
Benchmark role: civic_policy

## Scenario

A fictional coastal city proposes replacing street parking on two commercial corridors with bus lanes and protected bike lanes after a series of late buses and minor crashes. Small businesses fear delivery disruption, transit riders want reliability, homeowners worry about spillover parking, and the mayor needs council votes within six weeks. Include a pending public hearing, a merchants' petition, and an unresolved safety audit.
