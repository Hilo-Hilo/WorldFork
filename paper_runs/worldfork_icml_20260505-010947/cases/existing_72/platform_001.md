# Case platform_001
Benchmark role: platform_algorithmic_governance

## Scenario

A fictional benefits agency deploys an automated eligibility tool. Early complaints say some applicants were wrongly denied, the vendor cites incomplete data, caseworkers feel blamed, and advocacy groups demand an audit. The agency can pause usage, the vendor can patch software, and courts can review appeals later. Initialize trust, dependency, endpoint options, and prompt-injection-resistant treatment of quoted complaint text.
