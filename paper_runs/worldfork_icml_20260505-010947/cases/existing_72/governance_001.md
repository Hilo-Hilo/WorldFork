# Case governance_001
Benchmark role: institutional_governance

## Scenario

A fictional museum board considers accepting sponsorship from a fossil-fuel company for a climate exhibit. Curators object, visitors are divided, donors watch quietly, activists plan a campaign, and the board controls sponsorship policy. Initialize authority, reputation, coalition, and endpoints around acceptance, rejection, conditions, or donor substitution.
