# Case labor_002
Benchmark role: labor_economic_dependency

## Scenario

A food-delivery platform changes its pay formula in one metro area. Couriers claim take-home pay drops, restaurants fear slower service, customers see confusing fees, and the platform says the change rewards reliability. Couriers can organize boycotts and media pressure but cannot directly rewrite platform policy. Capture platform ownership leverage, exit options, economic elasticity, and branches around rollback, substitution, regulation, or durable stalemate.

## Expected Focus
- platform_leverage
- switching_costs
- coalition_durability
