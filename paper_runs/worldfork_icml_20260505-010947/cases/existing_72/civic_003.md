# Case civic_003
Benchmark role: civic_policy

## Scenario

A fictional library board considers removing weekend hours to cover a budget deficit. Parents, seniors, staff, fiscal watchdogs, and a quiet group of teen patrons have different stakes. The board can change hours, the city council controls budget transfers, and local media frames the issue as either austerity or mismanagement.
