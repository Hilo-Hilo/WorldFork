# Case corporate_004
Benchmark role: corporate_regulatory_endpoint

## Scenario

A dominant apartment-listing platform removes listings from brokers who refuse a new data-sharing agreement. Renters lose inventory, brokers accuse the platform of coercion, privacy advocates worry about tenant data, and housing regulators consider inquiry. Model platform leverage, broker exit costs, tenant dependency, and terminal endpoints including capitulation, substitution, regulation, or stalemate.
