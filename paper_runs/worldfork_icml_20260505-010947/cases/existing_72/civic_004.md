# Case civic_004
Benchmark role: civic_policy

## Scenario

A metropolitan transit authority announces that a privately operated ferry subsidy may end. Island residents depend on the service, environmental groups question emissions, ferry workers fear layoffs, and the contractor has a renewal clause. The authority can renegotiate but not unilaterally change labor terms. Track dependency edges, contract constraints, and plausible branches around subsidy extension, replacement service, strike threat, or service collapse.
