# Case labor_004
Benchmark role: labor_economic_dependency

## Scenario

A port trucking cooperative threatens a slow-down over new gate fees. Port management controls gate policy, shipping firms control contracts, drivers absorb wait times, and nearby residents care about emissions. A state agency can investigate but not instantly set fees. Model operational bottlenecks, indirect pressure events, and endpoints including fee revision, enforcement delay, contract shift, or failed mobilization.
