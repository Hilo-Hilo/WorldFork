# Case platform_006
Benchmark role: platform_algorithmic_governance

## Scenario

A marketplace for freelance tutors raises commission rates and introduces an AI matching score. High-rated tutors may stay, new tutors fear invisibility, families see price changes, and competitors advertise migration tools. The platform owns ranking and commission policy; tutors can organize, exit, or lobby regulators. Encode switching costs, endpoint priors, and second-order effects.
