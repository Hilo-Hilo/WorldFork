# Case health_006
Benchmark role: public_health_resource

## Scenario

A regional food bank introduces income verification after donor pressure. Some clients fear stigma, donors want accountability, volunteers worry about slower distribution, and partner churches disagree privately. The board can change policy, donors can redirect funds, and clients can avoid services. Model public silence, private disagreement, dependency, and branch hypotheses.
