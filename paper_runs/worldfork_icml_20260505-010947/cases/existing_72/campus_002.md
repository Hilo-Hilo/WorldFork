# Case campus_002
Benchmark role: campus_institutional_legitimacy

## Scenario

A university athletics department receives allegations that a star coach ignored hazing complaints. Athletes fear retaliation, alumni defend the coach, administrators hire outside counsel, and student journalists publish partial evidence. The university can suspend staff, investigators determine facts later, and donors influence reputation. Model uncertainty and possible merge paths around credible investigation outcomes.
