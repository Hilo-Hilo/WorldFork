# Case platform_004
Benchmark role: platform_algorithmic_governance

## Scenario

A school district pilots an AI attendance-risk score. Parents fear surveillance, counselors want early warning, students joke about gaming the system, and the vendor claims bias tests are proprietary. The school board can renew or cancel the pilot but cannot reveal vendor trade secrets without negotiation. Seed branch hypotheses around transparency, cancellation, opt-out, and normalized use.
