# Case civic_002
Benchmark role: civic_policy

## Scenario

A state housing agency threatens to withhold infrastructure grants from suburbs that miss new affordable-housing targets. Renters, homeowners, builders, local councils, and a regional governor all react. The statute gives the agency enforcement power but lawsuits could delay implementation. Model realistic authority, legal uncertainty, fiscal dependency, and possible endpoints: compliance, negotiated delay, court injunction, or partial exemption.
