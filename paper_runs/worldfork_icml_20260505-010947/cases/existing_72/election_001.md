# Case election_001
Benchmark role: election_legitimacy

## Scenario

A fictional city council race is thrown into uncertainty after a ballot-scanner outage delays results in three precincts. Election officials say paper ballots are secure, one campaign alleges selective delays, civic groups call for patience, and local influencers spread precinct-level rumors. Initialize trust, uncertainty, legal authority, and branches around certification, recount demand, misinformation persistence, or legitimacy repair.

- legitimacy
