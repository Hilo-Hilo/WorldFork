# Case corporate_002
Benchmark role: corporate_regulatory_endpoint

## Scenario

A regional pharmacy chain may be acquired by a national retailer. Workers fear scheduling cuts, patients worry about closures, local officials want guarantees, and antitrust review is possible. The companies can negotiate terms but regulators approve or block the deal. Seed endpoint priors around acquisition approval, divestiture, withdrawal, labor concessions, or community-benefit agreement.
