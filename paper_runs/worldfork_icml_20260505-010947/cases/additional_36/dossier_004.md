# Case dossier_004
Benchmark role: longform_dossier

Forecast question: After 10 simulated weeks, does the marketplace fee dispute resolve through rollback, phased compromise, seller exit, regulation, or stalemate?

## Scenario

A large online marketplace changes seller fees and ranking incentives. Independent sellers organize a protest while advertisers and logistics partners have different incentives. Track stakeholder leverage, switching costs, and regulatory risk.

## Source Packet

### Source 1: platform_policy_update / T-12 days

The platform raises referral fees by 2 percentage points for several categories and offers ranking boosts to sellers who buy bundled logistics.

### Source 2: seller_forum_digest / T-10 days

Top independent sellers discuss a one-week listing strike, but smaller sellers worry they cannot afford lost sales.

### Source 3: advertiser_brief / T-8 days

Major advertisers say marketplace traffic remains efficient and ask the platform to avoid visible checkout disruption.

### Source 4: logistics_partner_memo / T-7 days

Third-party logistics firms argue the bundled ranking boost unfairly ties search visibility to platform-owned fulfillment.

### Source 5: consumer_group_letter / T-4 days

A consumer group asks the competition authority to examine whether fee pass-through will raise prices.

### Source 6: platform_exec_email / T-2 days

Executives are open to category-specific transition periods but reject a full rollback in draft talking points.

## Candidate Endpoints
- full_rollback: Platform reverses the fee/ranking change
- phased_compromise: Platform keeps policy but delays or narrows implementation
- seller_exit_strike: Significant seller strike or exit disrupts listings
- regulatory_investigation: Competition authority opens formal investigation
- durable_stalemate: Policy remains contested without decisive resolution

## Expected Focus
- platform_leverage
- seller_coordination
- switching_costs
- regulatory_branch
