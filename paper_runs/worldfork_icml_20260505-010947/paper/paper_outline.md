# Paper Outline

Title: WorldFork: Auditable Branching Socio-Institutional World Models for LLM Forecasting Agents

## Thesis

WorldFork should be evaluated as an auditable forecasting substrate: it exposes branch lineage, endpoint ledgers, path mass, report provenance, unresolved uncertainty, and socio-institutional assumptions. Forecast accuracy claims must remain conditional until the full no-branch and branching WorldFork resolved runs are scored.

## Main Sections

1. Introduction: single-shot LLM forecasts are hard to audit; institutional forecasting needs inspectable assumptions.
2. Method: Big Bang initialization, ticks as caps rather than targets, branch policy, endpoint-ledger/path-mass stopping, mandatory resume/reuse, reports, social-state observability, and default OpenRouter `deepseek/deepseek-v4-flash` high-volume cohort/hero routing.
3. Benchmark and metrics: 108-card suite, 24 resolved retrospective cards, 18 long-horizon audit set, Brier/log/audit/social metrics.
4. Results: card QA, direct baselines, live initialization smoke, synchronous tick/report smoke, queued tick/report smoke, pending full matrix.
5. Limitations: retrospective leakage risk, synthetic audit scope, rubric-based social metrics, runtime/model dependence, no validated emotion claim.
6. Conclusion: WorldFork creates checkable forecast artifacts; accuracy claims require the remaining scored runs.
